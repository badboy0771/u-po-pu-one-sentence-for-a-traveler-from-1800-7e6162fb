**The entry** (148 characters, one sentence, 31 words, every word in use before 1800):

> It is a library in every house, where any book, letter, map, or song in the world can be found in a breath, and every reader may write back at once.

## Why this sentence

A traveler from 1800 knows a library, a house, a letter, a map and a song.
The sentence builds the whole modern internet out of those alone: a library
in every house (knowledge everywhere, not locked in one building), any book,
letter, map or song in the world (text, messages, places, music — the whole
of what the internet carries), found in a breath (instant, from anywhere),
and every reader may write back at once (it is two-way: everyone can answer,
not only read). No word in it was coined after the traveler's time — no
internet, computer, digital, online, website, email, phone, television,
radio, video, software, data, network, screen or pixel.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gates before writing: at most 200
characters, non-blank with no surrounding whitespace, exactly one sentence,
no post-1800 word from a forbidden list, and a read-back that matches the
sentence exactly. Run it from any directory:

```sh
node main.ts
```
